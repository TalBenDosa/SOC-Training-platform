Most SOC telemetry shows what happens inside your perimeter. Social media shows what is being prepared outside it.

Often days before an alert fires. But public does not mean unlimited: collection is bound by platform terms, privacy law and your own policy.

Open with the contrast between inside and outside the perimeter. Everything in this lesson is defensive, strictly public-source and passive.

Why a SOC looks at social media at all

Four use cases, each with a different owner and a different tolerance for false positives.

HACKTHESOCLesson 124Social media OSINT6 / 52

Four use cases that justify the effort

Brand and executive

Impersonation

Accounts copy the name, photo and bio of your CEO, support desk or brand for scams, fake giveaways and credential-harvesting links.

    Employees

    Oversharing and leaks

    Staff post badges, dashboards and job details; developers push code with keys inside. Scope: your domains, products, executives.

      Hacktivists

      Threat-actor monitoring

      Hacktivists announce targets, DDoS campaigns and leak claims publicly. Treat each claim as unverified until your telemetry confirms it.

        Roles

        Social-engineering exposure

        Public profiles reveal who works where, who reports to whom, vendors and travel. Defenders map this first to target training.

          Impersonation can also be the first hop of a business email compromise chain: a fake executive profile builds trust before a payment request arrives. The SOC role is to detect, assess, preserve evidence and hand a clean report to legal or the platform.
          HACKTHESOCLesson 124Social media OSINT7 / 52

          Three questions before you open any investigation

          If you cannot answer the second one, you are browsing, not investigating.

          • Which asset or person?Name what you are protecting before you collect anything.
          • What decision will it support?A takedown, training, an incident, or nothing.
          • What is the minimum I need?Collect only what makes that decision. Public is not unlimited.

          ATT&CK: Search Open Websites/Domains (T1593); on the adversary side, Social Media Accounts (T1585.001).

          These three questions are the habit to build. Return to them in the decision framework at the end of the lesson.

          Section 2 of 9

          Platform by platform: what is legitimately visible

          Each network exposes a different slice of identity, and each has its own terms.

          HACKTHESOCLesson 124Social media OSINT9 / 52

          Each platform answers a different defensive question

          PlatformTypical public signalsBest defensive question
          Microblogging (X)Handle, name, join date, bio, posts, replies, followers, verification badgeIs this account impersonating us, and when did it start?
          Professional network (LinkedIn)Name, headline, employer, role history, connections count, postsWho claims to work for us, and who over-shares org structure?
          Code hosting (GitHub)Public repos, commits with author email, issues, gists, org membershipDid a developer publish secrets or internal code?
          Forum aggregator (Reddit)Username, account age, karma, post and comment historyIs someone discussing our incident, selling access or doxxing staff?
          Facebook, InstagramPublic pages and posts, tagged photos, page creation date, admins by regionIs a fake page or ad using our logo?
          Messaging channels (Telegram)Public channel posts, member counts, forwarded-from metadata, creation cluesWhat are hacktivists or access brokers announcing about us?

          Read the current terms before building a workflow: rate limits, login walls and automation rules change often.

          The slice of identity tells you which question a platform can answer. Pick the platform from the question, not the other way round.
          HACKTHESOCLesson 124Social media OSINT10 / 52

          Two underused signals: page transparency and cross-platform

          Page and profile transparency

          Many networks show handle changes, the country of the admins or the page creation date. A ten-year-old brand whose page was created last month is a strong signal by itself.

            Cross-platform consistency

            A real executive profile is referenced by the corporate site, press releases and colleagues. An impersonator usually exists on one network with no inbound references.

              Both checks are passive and cost nothing. Ask which official page links to this account, and which page does not.
              HACKTHESOCLesson 124Social media OSINT11 / 52

              The main constraint on each platform

              PlatformMain constraint
              Microblogging (X)Developer terms limit automated scraping; use the official API or manual review
              LinkedInUser agreement prohibits scraping and fake profiles; login walls limit anonymous view
              GitHubAcceptable use rules; report secrets through the provider rather than abusing them
              RedditData API terms restrict bulk collection and redistribution
              Facebook, InstagramMeta terms prohibit automated collection without permission; private content is off limits
              TelegramJoining and interacting is an OPSEC and ethics decision; view public channels passively only
              Terms are contractual. Knowing the constraint before you start is part of the method, not an afterthought.
              HACKTHESOCLesson 124Social media OSINT12 / 52

              Four pitfalls when viewing profiles

              • Personal loginDo not log in with a personal account.
              • Befriending for visibilityDo not befriend, follow or message to see more: that is interaction and may breach the terms.
              • No result is not proofPrivacy settings, regional blocking and deleted content all hide things.
              • Verification badgesSome platforms sell them, and they mean different things on each.
              Never assume a profile is who it says it is. A badge is one weak signal, not a verdict.

              Section 3 of 9

              Judging authenticity: signals of a fake persona or bot

              No single signal proves a fake. Build a weighted picture and state calibrated confidence.

              HACKTHESOCLesson 124Social media OSINT14 / 52

              Profile and link signals: authentic pattern versus red flag

              Group the evidence into profile, behavior, network and content. Start with the profile.

              SignalAuthentic patternRed flag
              Account ageCreated years ago, history consistent with the claimed identityCreated days or weeks ago but claims to be an established brand or person
              Handle and nameMatches the official handle used on the corporate siteLookalike: swapped letters, added underscore, digit substitution, extra words
              Profile photoOriginal, unique photoStock or stolen image reused across unrelated accounts (confirm by reverse image search)
              LinksPoint to official domainsShortened links, newly registered lookalike domains, redirect chains
              The goal is a calibrated statement such as high confidence this account is an impersonator, not a claim of certainty.
              HACKTHESOCLesson 124Social media OSINT15 / 52

              Three tells to check by hand

              • Profile photoReverse image search for earlier copies. Synthetic faces appear nowhere earlier, so no match is not proof.
              • TimezonePlot post times by hour in UTC. A human shows a trough at local night; a flat shape means automation or shifts.
              • LanguageCompare with genuine posts: signature phrases, punctuation, hashtags, response time.

              A trough that matches a different region is a soft indicator only: VPNs change login location, not the operator's habits.

              Impersonators often copy bio text exactly but cannot match the voice across dozens of replies.
              HACKTHESOCLesson 124Social media OSINT16 / 52

              Behavior and network signals: authentic pattern versus red

              SignalAuthentic patternRed flag
              Posting patternIrregular human rhythm with gaps and mixed contentBursts at fixed intervals, posting round the clock, identical text with minor edits
              Followers and followingGradual growth, a mix of real interactionsFollowing bought in one spike, follows thousands but few follow back, default avatars
              EngagementReplies are specific, from varied accountsGeneric praise from accounts with the same creation date, likes with no discussion
              Language and timezoneMatches the claimed location and roleMismatched idioms, active hours fit a distant timezone, machine-translated phrasing
              These are what you will see again in the worked example: a follower spike with default avatars and templated replies.
              HACKTHESOCLesson 124Social media OSINT17 / 52

              Pitfalls: unusual is not the same as fake

              Real but odd

              Real people also have new accounts, odd hours and bought followers they did not ask for.

                Legitimate, not official

                A fan or parody account may be legitimate, protected speech. Bots are not always malicious: news feeds and status bots exist.

                  The real test

                  Does the account claim to be you or deceive your customers? That matters more than whether it looks unusual.

                    Overreacting to oddness leads to takedown requests against fans and critics. The deception test keeps you honest.

                    Section 4 of 9

                    Finding leaked organizational information

                    Defensive reconnaissance on yourself: start from an inventory, not from reading everything an employee posts.

                    HACKTHESOCLesson 124Social media OSINT19 / 52

                    Build a search inventory first

                    • DomainsYour primary and secondary domains.
                    • NamesProduct and project code names, internal tool names, executive names.
                    • FormatsThe format of your employee IDs and ticket numbers.
                    • RepositoriesThe names of your GitHub organizations.

                    These become keyword sets you can reuse and refine.

                    The inventory keeps scope narrow. You search for your organization's exposure, not for everything an employee does online.
                    HACKTHESOCLesson 124Social media OSINT20 / 52

                    Oversharing: individually harmless, together convincing

                    Desk and screen photos

                    Capture a dashboard URL, a login portal or a visible password note.

                      Badges and office photos

                      Show badge design and name, office layout and physical access details.

                        Travel and conference posts

                        Announce an executive's location and absence.

                          Job-adjacent posts

                          Name internal systems, e.g. the exact EDR, SIEM and cloud services in use.

                            None is a breach alone. The response is usually education through the manager or awareness program, not discipline.

                            Together these make a pretext convincing. Treat the finding as input to awareness training.
                            HACKTHESOCLesson 124Social media OSINT21 / 52

                            Found a live credential? The order of work

                            Do not test whether it works: logging in with a found credential is unauthorized access unless policy explicitly allows it.

                            1. 1Preserve evidence of the exposureCapture it first, before anything changes.
                            2. 2The owner rotates or revokesThe owning team revokes the credential, not you.
                            3. 3Check logs for useReview activity between exposure and rotation: now it is a SIEM investigation.
                            4. 4Request removalAsk for the content to come down, after the credential is dead.
                            Removal alone does not help: a key that was public may already be copied. Revocation is what closes the exposure.
                            HACKTHESOCLesson 124Social media OSINT22 / 52

                            Public repositories: where secrets hide

                            • What gets committedAPI keys, cloud access keys, database connection strings, private keys.
                            • Deleted is not goneA key removed from the latest version can still sit in commit history.
                            • Commit metadataLeaks the author's email address and sometimes an internal hostname.
                            • What to huntYour org's repos and known staff's personal repos, for key prefixes, high-entropy strings, environment config files.
                            • Turn on protectionProvider secret scanning and push protection should be on by default.
                            • Whose data is it?Our systems, or a person's private life? If private, stop and escalate.
                            Ask also: is the secret still valid, and who owns it? The last question, what logs show its use, links social-media findings to a real incident investigation.

                            Section 5 of 9

                            Preserving evidence before it disappears

                            Posts are deleted and accounts suspended within hours. Preserve first, analyze second.

                            HACKTHESOCLesson 124Social media OSINT24 / 52

                            What a defensible capture records

                            Keep a simple evidence log so each artifact has an ID.

                            • Full URLExactly where the item was captured.
                            • Handle and numeric IDHandles can change; the numeric account identifier usually does not.
                            • Post text and timestampsThe exact text and every visible timestamp.
                            • Engagement countsAs they stood at capture time.
                            • Analyst and UTC timeWho captured it, with capture date and time in UTC.
                            • Tool and methodSo another analyst can reproduce it.
                            A bookmark is not evidence. If you keep only a bookmark you will have nothing for legal, the platform or the incident file.
                            HACKTHESOCLesson 124Social media OSINT25 / 52

                            Four layered capture methods

                            1. 1Screenshot with contextFull page with address bar, system clock and post. Consistent window size, lossless PNG.
                            2. 2Web archiveA third party holds an independent timestamped copy. Public pages only; the archive is publicly viewable.
                            3. 3Saved page or print-to-PDFText stays searchable. Save the HTML source too: it holds identifiers not visible on screen.
                            4. 4Media downloadWhere terms allow, store the original file for reverse image search and metadata review.
                            A cropped image of just a post is easy to dispute. Avoid archiving sensitive third-party personal data, since the archive becomes publicly viewable.
                            HACKTHESOCLesson 124Social media OSINT26 / 52

                            Hash every file the moment it is saved

                            A hash is an integrity fingerprint you write into the evidence log.

                            • Write the hash into the evidence log immediately
                            • Recompute later: a matching value shows the file is unchanged
                            • Store originals read-only and work on copies
                            The file name here is only a placeholder. Hashing does not encrypt anything, so access control is still needed, and it says nothing about who wrote the original post.
                            HACKTHESOCLesson 124Social media OSINT27 / 52

                            Chain of custody and the limits of a screenshot

                            Chain of custody

                            Record who holds each file, where it is stored and who accessed it. Use a restricted location, not shared chat.

                              Screenshots are not conclusive

                              They can be forged, so they only corroborate. The archive copy and hashes strengthen them.

                                Do not over-preserve

                                One impersonating account does not justify downloading its entire follower list.

                                  Capture before you report

                                  The platform report may trigger removal. A takedown helps only if the evidence file exists first.

                                    Browser extensions that rewrite pages can alter what you captured, so use a clean research browser profile.

                                    Section 6 of 9

                                    OPSEC and ethics: look without being seen or causing harm

                                    Keep the investigator invisible, the target undisturbed and the collection minimal.

                                    HACKTHESOCLesson 124Social media OSINT29 / 52

                                    Research identity versus personal account

                                    Research account and

                                    A dedicated analyst identity not tied to your personal profile, real name or your employer's brand.

                                    • Separate browser profile or isolated VM
                                    • Network egress approved by your organization
                                    • Check whether the platform notifies profile viewers

                                    Your personal account

                                    Never use it. It can expose your own friends, your employer and your location to the person you are investigating.

                                      Some professional networks notify members who viewed their profile. Check how the platform treats a viewer before you open a suspicious profile.
                                      HACKTHESOCLesson 124Social media OSINT30 / 52

                                      Passive means passive

                                      Do not follow, friend, like, comment, vote, join private groups or send messages.

                                      • It tips off the actorThey may delete content, rotate to a new account, or retaliate against whoever contacted them.
                                      • It may breach termsInteraction can violate the platform's terms.
                                      • It contaminates evidenceYour interaction changes the very record you are documenting.
                                      • Public channelsRead them without joining. If joining is required, escalate to legal rather than improvise.
                                      • No pretextingFake personas or false identities to extract information breach most terms and can violate privacy and fraud laws.
                                      This holds even when your goal is defensive. Authorized red-team social-engineering assessments are a different activity with written scope and consent.
                                      HACKTHESOCLesson 124Social media OSINT31 / 52

                                      Minimization: collect only what answers the question

                                      PracticeWhat it means
                                      Scope to the reportFor impersonation: the account, posts and links, not anyone's family or personal life
                                      Keep personal data outOut of tickets, and redacted from reports where possible
                                      Set a retention periodDecide how long evidence is kept and apply it at case close
                                      Public is still personal dataGDPR treats public data about identifiable people as personal data
                                      A lawful basis is neededPurpose limitation and secure storage apply even when the data was public
                                      Minimization protects both the people in the data and your organization from a data-protection problem.
                                      HACKTHESOCLesson 124Social media OSINT32 / 52

                                      Hard legal lines you never cross

                                      Do not break in

                                      No private accounts, no guessing passwords, no leaked credentials, no bypassing login walls or rate limits by technical tricks.

                                        Do not buy or dox

                                        Do not purchase stolen data. Never publish personal details of an actor or an employee, even a malicious actor.

                                          Hand it on

                                          Give the evidence to legal counsel, the platform or law enforcement.

                                            Test: would I be comfortable if this collection were described in a court filing or an employee-facing privacy notice?

                                            If the answer is uncertain, pause and ask. A documented, lawful purpose is the minimum.

                                            Section 7 of 9

                                            Tooling at a concept level and the limits of automation

                                            Tools speed up collection but do not change the rules. Think in categories, not product names.

                                            HACKTHESOCLesson 124Social media OSINT34 / 52

                                            Seven tool categories and the limit on each

                                            CategoryDefensive useLimit
                                            Manual search operatorsFind lookalike accounts and mentions of your domainsSlow but fully within terms
                                            Official APIsScheduled monitoring of brand keywordsRate limits, paid tiers, use-case review, storage restrictions
                                            Brand-protection and threat-intel servicesScale across platforms, with a takedown workflowVendor contract covers legality; verify their method
                                            Archiving toolsEvidence preservationCannot see logged-in content
                                            Reverse image and media toolsDetect stolen photosWeak for synthetic faces and heavily edited images
                                            Code-hosting secret scannersFind exposed keys in your orgRun on your own repos or via provider features, not mass harvesting
                                            Network-graph and link analysisShow coordinated clusters of accounts and domainsOnly as good as the data collected
                                            Specific tools come and go while the constraints stay. Learn the category and its limit.
                                            HACKTHESOCLesson 124Social media OSINT35 / 52

                                            Why automation is constrained

                                            • Platform termsMost prohibit scraping around official interfaces, automated viewing accounts, and volume collection without authorization.
                                            • ConsequencesAccount bans, blocked IP ranges, cease-and-desist letters, contract claims.
                                            • Evidence riskWhat you produced may be attacked as improperly obtained.
                                            • The law variesScraping rulings differ by jurisdiction, so never rely on 'it was public, so it was fine'.
                                            Your policy should not rest on the public-so-fine argument. Contract and jurisdiction both matter.
                                            HACKTHESOCLesson 124Social media OSINT36 / 52

                                            A sensible approach to tooling

                                            1. 1Official APIs and licensed providersUse them for continuous monitoring.
                                            2. 2Manual passive reviewUse it for individual investigations.
                                            3. 3Document approved toolsList each tool, the data it may collect and who approved it.
                                            4. 4Rate-limit yourselfNever run bots that log in, follow or interact.
                                            The documented list is what lets you show a reviewer your method.
                                            HACKTHESOCLesson 124Social media OSINT37 / 52

                                            Machine assistance is a lead, not evidence

                                            Where models help

                                            Triage large volumes of text, translate posts, cluster similar accounts.

                                              Where they fail

                                              They hallucinate, mis-translate slang and reflect bias. Verify every claim against the original source. Never paste sensitive internal data into external tools without approval.

                                                Ask about any tool

                                                Four questions before you adopt it.

                                                • Where does the data come from?
                                                • Is that collection permitted by the platform?
                                                • Where is it stored, and for how long?
                                                • Can I reproduce it and show my method?
                                                Model output goes into a report only after you have checked it against the original post or page.

                                                Section 8 of 9

                                                Worked example: impersonation of a support account

                                                A customer forwards a screenshot: a fake support desk asks them to verify their account through a link.

                                                HACKTHESOCLesson 124Social media OSINT39 / 52

                                                Six steps from report to follow-through

                                                Everything uses only public data, viewed from the research account.

                                                1. 1Scope and triageIs this account ours? Not on the official social inventory, so unauthorized.
                                                2. 2Preserve firstPassive captures, archive, HTML source, account ID, UTC times, SHA-256 hashes.
                                                3. 3Assess authenticity signalsWeigh age, handle, photo, followers, posting pattern, link, cross-references.
                                                4. 4Conclude with confidenceSeparate what is observed from what is inferred.
                                                5. 5File the takedown reportOfficial impersonation channel; legal and support notified.
                                                6. 6Follow throughBlock the domain, search logs, monitor for re-registration.
                                                In step 1 the official site links to a different handle, which is what makes the preliminary assessment unauthorized. Note that you capture without following or interacting.
                                                HACKTHESOCLesson 124Social media OSINT40 / 52

                                                The authenticity signals in this case

                                                SignalObservationWeight
                                                Account ageCreated nine days agoStrong
                                                HandleOfficial handle with one letter swapped and a trailing wordStrong
                                                Bio and photoLogo copied from our site; reverse image search finds the original only on our domainMedium
                                                Followers4,000 followers, almost all created in the same two-day window, default avatarsStrong
                                                Posting pattern60 near-identical replies in 3 hours; no posts at night UTC+0 to +2Medium
                                                LinkShortened URL resolving to a newly registered lookalike domainStrong
                                                Cross-referencesNot linked from our site, not followed by employeesMedium

                                                Check the link's registration date and hosting with passive lookups. Never enter credentials or interact from your workstation.

                                                Pass the destination domain to the email and web security teams for blocking and any needed analysis. Weight is what each signal contributes to the picture.
                                                HACKTHESOCLesson 124Social media OSINT41 / 52

                                                Conclusion: high confidence, with limits stated

                                                Observed

                                                A nine-day-old lookalike handle, a synchronized follower spike, templated replies and a lookalike link domain.

                                                  Inferred

                                                  High confidence this is a deceptive impersonation account, likely harvesting credentials.

                                                    Unknown

                                                    The actor's identity and location. The posting hours are only a soft indicator.

                                                      Keep fact, inference and unknown separate in the ticket. This is the discipline that avoids naming a person or country from weak signals.
                                                      HACKTHESOCLesson 124Social media OSINT42 / 52

                                                      Takedown report and follow-through

                                                      1. 1Assemble the reportCase summary, official-handle evidence, policy violated, archived URLs.
                                                      2. 2Attach the evidenceHashed screenshots, harmful link, affected customers reduced to handles.
                                                      3. 3Submit officiallyUse the platform's impersonation channel and record the submission time.
                                                      4. 4Warn real customersNotify legal and support so the genuine account posts a public warning.
                                                      5. 5Block and searchBlock the domain; search mail and proxy logs for employee visits.
                                                      6. 6Monitor and closeWatch for re-registration under a new handle; retain evidence per policy.
                                                      Customers are minimized to handles to keep personal data out of the report. Closing the loop includes checking telemetry for impact.

                                                      Common mistakes and a decision framework

                                                      Most failures are process failures, not technical ones. A short framework prevents them.

                                                      HACKTHESOCLesson 124Social media OSINT44 / 52

                                                      Six predictable mistakes and what each costs

                                                      MistakeWhat goes wrong
                                                      Interacting to learn moreGives away that someone is watching and may breach terms; the actor deletes or moves
                                                      Reporting before preservingAfter suspension you may have only a customer's cropped screenshot
                                                      Over-collectionPrivacy risk, storage burden, data-protection problem; rarely improves the finding
                                                      Attributing from weak signalsNight activity or bad writing proves no region or actor: name facts, then confidence
                                                      Treating public as unrestrictedLocked communities, volume scraping, testing a found credential are out of bounds
                                                      Confusing impersonation with parodyOverbroad takedowns of satire or criticism cause reputational damage; consult legal
                                                      Each row maps to a section of this lesson. Mistake 2 is the costliest in practice because the evidence cannot be recreated.
                                                      HACKTHESOCLesson 124Social media OSINT45 / 52

                                                      Decision framework, part 1: before you collect

                                                      1. 11. PurposeWhat decision will this support, and who owns it?
                                                      2. 22. Legality and termsIs the source public, and is my method allowed by platform and policy?
                                                      3. 33. Passive accessAm I viewing from the research account with no interaction?
                                                      4. 44. MinimizeWhat is the smallest data set that answers the question?
                                                      Steps 1 to 4 are the questions from the start of the lesson, now as a gate before any collection.
                                                      HACKTHESOCLesson 124Social media OSINT46 / 52

                                                      Decision framework, part 2: capture, judge, act

                                                      5. Preserve

                                                      Timestamped, hashed, archived evidence before any report or action.

                                                        6. Assess

                                                        Weigh multiple authenticity signals; state your confidence and its limits.

                                                          7. Act through channels

                                                          Platform report, legal, IT or security teams, not personal confrontation.

                                                            8. Close the loop

                                                            Check telemetry for impact, update awareness material, monitor for reappearance, apply retention.

                                                              The same eight steps apply to impersonation, leak hunting and threat-actor monitoring.
                                                              HACKTHESOCLesson 124Social media OSINT47 / 52
                                                              A SOC analyst gathers and preserves facts. It does not run vigilante investigations.

                                                              If a case involves harassment or threats to a named individual, or minors, stop and escalate to legal, HR or law enforcement liaison.

                                                              End on the boundary. Knowing when to stop and hand off is part of the job.

                                                              Key takeaways

                                                              1. 01Social media shows what is prepared outside your perimeter: impersonation, oversharing, threat-actor claims and social-engineering exposure.
                                                              2. 02No single signal proves a fake account: weigh profile, behavior, network and content, then state calibrated confidence.
                                                              3. 03Leak hunting is reconnaissance on yourself: preserve, have the owner revoke the credential, check logs, then request removal.
                                                              4. 04Preserve before you report: full-context capture, archive, UTC time, SHA-256 hash and chain of custody.
                                                              5. 05Look passively from a research account: no interaction, no pretexting, minimal collection, no doxxing.
                                                              6. 06Use official APIs and licensed providers, treat model output as a lead, and escalate rather than improvise.
